Profile

Commercial control, from measurement to final account.

I'm a Senior Quantity Surveyor with 13+ years on EPC, EPCM and FEED projects in oil & gas, power, petrochemical, buildings and infrastructure.

My strength is post-contract commercial control: payment certification, variations and FEWOs, subcontract administration and cash-flow forecasting, backed by accurate QTO, MTO and BOQ work.

I've worked on both sides of the table: as subcontractor and contractor QS in Malaysia and Saudi Arabia, and on the EPCM side for ADNOC in Abu Dhabi.
